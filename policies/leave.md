# Leave Policy

## 1.1 Scope

This policy applies to all employees of Harbor & Pine Stores, including store, warehouse and head office staff. Temporary agency workers follow the leave terms of their agency.

## 1.2 Leave Year

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Employees who join or leave during the year receive a pro rata entitlement based on the number of full months worked.

## 2.1 Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per leave year. Part-time employees receive the same entitlement calculated pro rata to their contracted hours. After five years of continuous service, the entitlement increases by 2 days.

## 2.2 Requesting Annual Leave

Annual leave must be requested through the staff portal at least 14 days before the first day of leave. Managers respond within 5 working days. Requests are approved in the order they are received, subject to store staffing needs.

## 2.3 Blackout Periods

Store employees may not take annual leave between 20 November and 31 December without written approval from the Area Manager. This restriction does not apply to head office staff.

## 2.4 Carrying Over Leave

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next leave year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost. Any further carry-over requires approval from the HR Director.

## 3.1 Sick Leave Reporting

Employees who are too ill to work must call their manager at least 2 hours before their shift starts. A text message or email is not sufficient notice. Employees must update their manager on each day of absence unless a medical note covers the period.

## 3.2 Sick Pay

Employees with at least 6 months of service receive full pay for up to 10 working days of sickness in any rolling 12-month period. A medical note is required for any absence longer than 5 consecutive working days.

## 4.1 Parental Leave

Employees who give birth or adopt as the primary carer receive 26 weeks of leave, of which the first 16 weeks are at full pay. Secondary carers receive 4 weeks at full pay. Employees must give at least 8 weeks of written notice before leave begins.

## 4.2 Bereavement Leave

Employees receive up to 5 days of paid leave following the death of an immediate family member, and 1 day for the death of another relative or close friend. Requests should be made to the manager as soon as is practical.
