# Store Opening Checklist

## 1.1 Who Opens the Store

Stores are opened by two people, one of whom must be a keyholder. No employee may enter the store alone before opening. If the second person has not arrived within 15 minutes, the keyholder must wait outside and call the Area Manager.

## 1.2 Arrival Time

The opening team arrives 45 minutes before the store opens to customers. Keyholders must not share keys or alarm codes with anyone.

## 2.1 Entering the Building

Before entering, walk around the outside of the building and check for broken glass or forced doors. If anything looks wrong, do not enter. Move to a safe place, call the police, and then call the Area Manager. Once inside, disarm the alarm within 60 seconds of opening the door.

## 2.2 Safety Walk

Walk the sales floor and stockroom to check for spills, blocked exits and damaged fixtures. Confirm that all fire exits open freely and that the first aid kit is in place. Record the walk in the opening log.

## 3.1 Tills and Cash

Collect till floats from the safe and count each float with a second person present. Each till starts the day with a float of 150 pounds. Log on to each till and run a test transaction before opening.

## 3.2 Systems Check

Check that the card terminals, price scanners and security cameras are working. Report any fault to the IT Service Desk on extension 4100 before opening.

## 4.1 Sales Floor Preparation

Switch on lighting, music and digital displays. Check that promotional signs match the current price list, and remove any expired promotional material. Fill gaps on the shelves with stock from the morning delivery.

## 4.2 Team Briefing and Opening the Doors

Hold a 5-minute team briefing 10 minutes before opening to share the daily sales target and any safety notices. Unlock the customer doors at the published opening time, and not before. Sign the opening log once every step is complete.
