# Store Closing Checklist

## 1.1 Who Closes the Store

Stores are closed by two people, one of whom must be a keyholder. Both people must leave the building together. No employee may remain in the store alone after closing.

## 1.2 Closing Announcements

Make a customer announcement 15 minutes before closing and again 5 minutes before closing. Customer doors are locked at the published closing time, and customers already inside are allowed to finish their purchases.

## 2.1 Cashing Up

Each till is counted by its assigned employee with a second person present. Reset each till to a float of 150 pounds and place the float in the safe. All takings go into a sealed cash bag, and the bag number is recorded in the cash log.

## 2.2 Securing the Safe

The safe must be locked and checked by the keyholder. Cash must never be left in tills overnight. Till drawers are left open and empty so that they can be seen from outside.

## 3.1 Sales Floor Check

Walk the whole store, including fitting rooms, toilets and stockrooms, to confirm that no customers remain. Check that all fire exits are shut and that no stock blocks them. Clear away any spills.

## 3.2 Equipment Shutdown

Switch off music, digital displays and non-essential lighting. Leave the security lighting and refrigeration units running. Card terminals stay on overnight to receive updates.

## 4.1 Setting the Alarm

Set the alarm and leave within 60 seconds. If the alarm will not set, call the alarm monitoring centre on 0800 555 0142. Do not leave the building until the fault is resolved or the Area Manager has given instructions.

## 4.2 Leaving the Building

Lock all doors, then test them from the outside. Sign the closing log, recording the time and both names. If anyone suspicious is near the building, go back inside, lock the door and call the police.
