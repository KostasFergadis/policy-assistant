# Code of Conduct

## 1.1 Purpose

This code sets out how everyone at Harbor & Pine Stores is expected to behave at work. It applies during working hours, at work events, and whenever an employee represents the company.

## 2.1 Respect at Work

Harassment, bullying and discrimination are not tolerated. This covers unwanted comments, jokes, messages and physical contact. Complaints are treated seriously and confidentially.

## 2.2 Dress and Appearance

Store employees must wear the company uniform and a visible name badge while on shift. Uniforms must be clean and in good repair. Head office staff dress in smart casual clothing.

## 3.1 Gifts and Hospitality

Employees must not accept gifts worth more than 25 pounds from a supplier or customer. All gifts of any value must be recorded in the gift register. Cash or gift cards must never be accepted, whatever the amount.

## 3.2 Conflicts of Interest

Employees must tell their manager in writing about any personal or financial interest that could affect their work. This includes a second job with a competitor or supplier, or a close relationship with someone they manage.

## 3.3 Employee Discount

Employees receive a 20 percent discount on full-price items. The discount is for personal use and for immediate family only. Employees must not process their own purchases or those of their family at the till.

## 4.1 Social Media

Employees must not post confidential company information, or photographs of stockrooms or back-office areas, on social media. Only the Communications Team may speak on behalf of the company.

## 4.2 Phones During Shifts

Personal phones must not be used on the sales floor. Employees may use their phones during breaks and in staff areas. Exceptions can be agreed with a manager for family emergencies.

## 5.1 Reporting Concerns

Concerns about illegal or unethical behaviour should be raised with a manager or through the confidential Speak Up line on 0800 555 0177. Reports can be made anonymously. No one will be disadvantaged for raising a concern in good faith.

## 5.2 Breaches of This Code

Breaches of this code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal. Serious cases of theft, violence or harassment may be treated as gross misconduct.
