# Expense Policy

## 1.1 General Principles

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable costs that they incur while on company business. Expenses must be necessary, supported by a receipt, and approved by the employee's line manager.

## 2.1 Submitting a Claim

Claims must be submitted through the expenses portal within 30 days of the date of purchase. Claims submitted after 30 days will not be paid unless approved by the Finance Director. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll.

## 2.2 Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every claim. A card statement is not accepted as a receipt. If a receipt is lost, the employee must complete a missing receipt form, and this is allowed for amounts up to 15 pounds only.

## 3.1 Travel

Rail travel must be booked in standard class through the company travel portal at least 7 days ahead where possible. Flights require approval from a director. Taxis may be claimed only when public transport is not available or when travelling after 9pm.

## 3.2 Mileage

Employees who use their own car for business travel may claim 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year, and 25 pence per mile after that. Normal commuting between home and the usual place of work cannot be claimed.

## 3.3 Hotels

Hotel stays must be booked through the company travel portal. The limit is 120 pounds per night in London and 90 pounds per night elsewhere, including breakfast where available.

## 4.1 Meals

When working away overnight, employees may claim up to 35 pounds per day for meals. A day trip of more than 5 hours away from the usual place of work allows a claim of up to 10 pounds for lunch. Alcohol cannot be claimed.

## 4.2 Client Entertainment

Client entertainment must be approved in advance by a director. The claim must list the name and company of every person attending. The limit is 50 pounds per person.

## 5.1 Items That Cannot Be Claimed

The following cannot be claimed: fines and parking penalties, personal toiletries, personal entertainment, and travel insurance for personal trips.
