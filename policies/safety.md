# Workplace Safety

## 1.1 Responsibilities

Every employee is responsible for working safely and for reporting hazards. The Store Manager is the responsible person for health and safety in each store. Each store must have at least one trained first aider on every shift.

## 2.1 Reporting Accidents and Near Misses

All accidents, injuries and near misses must be recorded in the incident log before the end of the shift, however minor they appear. Any injury that requires hospital treatment must be reported to the Safety Team within 1 hour by calling the safety line on extension 4400.

## 2.2 First Aid

First aid kits are kept in the staff room and behind the main customer service desk. Kits are checked on the first Monday of each month by the duty manager. Used items must be replaced within 24 hours.

## 3.1 Fire Evacuation

When the fire alarm sounds, stop work immediately and leave by the nearest marked exit. Do not use lifts and do not return for personal belongings. Direct customers to the exits and gather at the assembly point shown on the store floor plan. Only the fire warden may confirm that it is safe to return.

## 3.2 Fire Exits and Drills

Fire exits and the routes leading to them must be kept clear at all times. Stock must never be stored in front of an exit, even briefly. Each store carries out a fire drill every 6 months and records the result in the safety file.

## 4.1 Manual Handling

Employees must complete manual handling training before moving stock. No employee should lift a load over 20 kg alone. Use a trolley or pallet truck, or ask a colleague to help. Heavy items must be stored between knee and shoulder height.

## 4.2 Ladders and Working at Height

Only store ladders with a green inspection tag may be used. Ladders must be inspected every 3 months. Employees must not stand on the top two steps, and must never climb shelving or stand on boxes or crates.

## 5.1 Wet Floors and Spills

Spills must be dealt with immediately. The employee who finds a spill stays with it or places a yellow wet floor sign beside it until it has been cleaned. Signs may be removed only once the floor is fully dry.

## 5.2 Personal Protective Equipment

Safety footwear with reinforced toes must be worn in warehouses and stockrooms. Cut-resistant gloves must be worn when using a box cutter. The company provides this equipment free of charge, and damaged items are replaced on request.
